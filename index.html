🛡️ Cyber Awareness Lab

Lessons for recognizing phishing and social-engineering tricks

Module 1 — Wifi Portal

See, step by step, how a scam "free wifi" sign-in page tries to trick you — and why it's never safe to type a real password into one. 100% simulated, nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.

Module 2 — Spot the Phish

A scored quiz with realistic (but fake-brand) login and wifi screens. Click the red flags and find out how sharp your eye is.

For teachers & facilitators

This site is a closed, static training simulation. Module 1 never transmits, stores, or logs anything a student types — every "result" is generated instantly in the student's own browser purely to illustrate the lesson. There is no backend, no database, and no network request carrying form data. Use it in a classroom or assembly as a live walkthrough, then debrief with Module 2 as a quiz.

Educational simulation only. No real credentials are ever requested, stored, or transmitted.
